Contact

Bug Reports and Feature Requests

openMSX Issue Tracker
You can report bugs or request new features for openMSX itself in the issue tracker on GitHub. You need a (free) log-in on GitHub to get access.

Discussion Forum

openMSX forum on MSX Resource Center
Discussions on openMSX, Catapult and C-BIOS.

IRC

#openMSX on Libera.Chat
You'll find most of the developers here on a regular basis. Don't hesitate to visit, don't hesitate to ask anything openMSX related. Remarks, suggestions, ideas are most welcome!
It's also reachable via webchat.
If you don't get a reply immediately, please stick around for a while, or use one of the other contact options. The majority of the developers live in time zone GMT+1. You may get no response if you contact them in the middle of their night...

Discord

openMSX on Discord
The best place to ask your question there is the #openmsx-irc-bridge channel, which is connected to our IRC channel.

Mailing Lists

SourceForge openMSX mailing lists
These are hardly used anymore, so you will probably get a faster reply via one of the options above. If you're a regular user and want to discuss openMSX and possible problems, join our openmsx-user mailing list. If you want to address the openMSX developers directly, post a message to the openmsx-devel mailing list. An archive of old messages can also be found there.
